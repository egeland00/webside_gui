about_me

Hey — I'm Orjan, a cybersecurity and network enthusiast. I'm currently studying Cyber Security at Gokstad Akademiet and hold an MSc in Computing from the University of Roehampton (2023). Genuine passion for everything involving packets, ports, and things that probably shouldn't be poked at.

My world sits at the intersection of network security, ethical hacking, and Python automation. I spend a fair amount of time on TryHackMe, working through challenges that range from beginner to "why is this even a thing."
